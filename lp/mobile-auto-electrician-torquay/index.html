Common electrical problems we get called out to

If yours isn't here, call and describe it — auto electrical and 12V faults are the whole job.

Car won't start

Cranks and won't fire, a single click, or nothing at all. Starting, ignition and immobiliser circuits tested on site.

Flat again after a few days

Parasitic drain testing to find the circuit, module or added accessory pulling current once the vehicle is locked.

Charging system faults

Battery, alternator and charging-circuit testing when the battery light is on or the vehicle keeps going flat.

Warning lights on the dash

Codes read and interpreted, then the circuit actually tested. A code points at a circuit, not automatically at a part.

Trailer lights not working

Indicators, brake lights or tail lights dead on the trailer — socket, wiring and vehicle-side circuit all checked.

Caravan or camper with no power

12V faults in the van — charging from the vehicle, battery, DC-DC charger, fusing and wiring traced back to the source.

An accessory stopped working

Dash cam, reverse camera, head unit, fridge or UHF that has died or drops out — supply, earth and wiring tested.

Modules not communicating

No comms with the scan tool, network errors, or modules dropping off the bus — common on later European vehicles.

European vehicle specialist diagnostics

European vehicles carry more control modules and far more network traffic than most. A generic code reader frequently can't see far enough in — which is how parts end up replaced on a guess.

Mercedes-Benz BMW Audi Volkswagen

The faults that get stuck

  • Warning lights that return after a reset
  • Difficult, intermittent electrical faults
  • Communication errors between control modules
  • Modules that won't respond or drop off the network
  • Drain and quiescent-current faults tied to one module

How we work through it

  • Full multi-module scan, not just the engine ECU
  • Live data and circuit testing to confirm the real fault
  • CAN-bus and network testing where modules aren't answering
  • Coding and programming-related diagnosis where the job calls for it
  • Findings explained before any repair is quoted
